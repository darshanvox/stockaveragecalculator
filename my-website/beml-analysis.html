BEML Ltd: Decoding the 2-for-1 Stock Split & Record Order Book

Published on September 20, 2026 • By Analyst

Bharat Earth Movers Limited (BEML) has drawn significant market attention following rapid corporate updates and multi-crore infrastructural wins.

1. The Historic Stock Split

BEML implemented a 2-for-1 stock split on November 3, 2025. This move divided the face value of the shares from ₹10 to ₹5, effectively doubling the outstanding share count while making trading liquidity accessible for standard retail accounts.

2. Dividend Profile & Key Dates

The enterprise continues its historical payout trend, locking in a final corporate dividend distribution of ₹12.28 per equity share for the fiscal timeline. The upcoming official record cutoff date is firmly established for September 22, 2026.

3. Operational Catalysts

Financially, Q1 FY27 revenue operations bounded forward by 29.3% YoY to land at ₹820 crore. Growth is heavily anchored by the groundbreaking ₹5,400 crore operational contract secured via the National High Speed Rail Corporation Ltd to engineer rolling stock formations for the premier Mumbai-Ahmedabad bullet train high-speed system.
